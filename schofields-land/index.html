About Us

Valrico's Tree & Property Specialists

Schofields Land Management & Tree Removal LLC is a locally owned tree service based in Valrico, serving Brandon and east Hillsborough County. We handle property-focused work from start to finish — tree removal, trimming and pruning, stump grinding, yard and debris cleanup, and ongoing lawn maintenance. We run real equipment, including a bucket truck and stump grinder, so big jobs get done safely instead of being turned down. Whether it's one dangerous oak over your roof or a full property reset, we show up, work clean, and haul it all away. Free estimates on every job.
