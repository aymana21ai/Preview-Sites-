About Us

Cape Coral's Trusted In-Home Pet Sitter

Happy Wagging Tails is in-home pet boarding run by Miriam, right here in Cape Coral. Your pet doesn't go into a kennel run or a stacked cage — they stay in a real home with a fenced yard, a couch to nap on, and someone who's actually around all day. That's why clients keep coming back: dogs settle in fast, anxious pets do better, and owners get photo updates while they're away instead of wondering. Over 130 pet parents have left reviews averaging 4.9 stars, and most of them mention the same thing — Miriam treats their pets like her own. Dogs and cats are both welcome, from weekend trips to extended stays.
