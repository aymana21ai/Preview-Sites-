About Us

Dunedin's Professional Moving Team

Ready To Move Hauling Services is a locally owned, licensed and insured moving company serving Dunedin and the greater Pinellas area. We handle local and long-distance moves with careful, professional crews who treat your belongings like their own — packing, loading, furniture, heavy items, and everything in between. We're licensed with the FMCSA (USDOT 3477868) and carry full cargo coverage, so your move is protected start to finish. Open 24 hours, with a free, no-obligation estimate on every job.
