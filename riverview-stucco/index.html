About Us

Riverview's Stucco Specialists

Riverview Stucco Repair Contractors is a stucco and exterior finishing company serving Riverview, Brandon, Gibsonton, and the greater Tampa area. We specialize in stucco and siding repair, refinishing, and installation — everything from hairline crack patching and water-damage repair to full exterior re-stucco and multi-story new construction. The part most homeowners care about is the finish: we match and blend the new stucco to your existing texture so the patch doesn't stand out. Kevin and the crew handle residential and new-construction work, and every job starts with a free estimate.

Our Services

Our Stucco Services

Stucco Crack & Patch Repair

Hairline cracks, spider cracks, and damaged sections cut out, patched, and sealed before moisture gets behind the wall and causes bigger problems.

Texture Matching & Blending

The detail that makes or breaks a repair — we match your existing texture and blend the new work in so you can't tell where the patch ends.

New Construction Stucco

Full stucco application on new builds and additions, including multi-story homes — scratch, brown, and finish coats applied to spec.

Full Exterior Re-Stucco

Tired, cracked, or dated exteriors stripped and re-stuccoed from the ground up for a clean, uniform finish across the whole house.

Stucco & Siding Refinishing

Refinishing and re-coating that restores your home's exterior and gets it prepped and ready for paint.

Water & Storm Damage Repair

Florida weather is hard on stucco. We repair water intrusion, storm damage, and rot-related failures and seal it back up properly.
