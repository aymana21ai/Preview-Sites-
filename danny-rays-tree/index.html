About Us

Leesburg's Climbing Tree Specialists

Danny Ray's Tree Service is a local, owner-operated tree company serving Leesburg and Lake County. What sets us apart is what we specialize in: climbing and confined-area removals. When a tree is boxed in between a house, a fence, and a power line with no room for a bucket truck or crane, that's the job we take. Danny climbs it and brings it down piece by piece, safely. Customers consistently mention three things — he shows up when he says he will, he communicates clearly, and he stays until the job is finished, even if that means working past dark. We also handle trimming, palm work, storm damage, and stump grinding, and we're reachable 24 hours for emergencies.
