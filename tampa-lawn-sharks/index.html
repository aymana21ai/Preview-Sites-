About Us

Tampa Bay's Lawn & Landscape Pros

Tampa Lawn Sharks is a locally owned lawn care and landscaping company with over 10 years of experience serving St. Petersburg, Tampa, and the greater Tampa Bay area. Owner Curtis and his crew handle it all for both commercial and residential properties — weekly lawn service, custom landscape design, our signature landscape curbing, mulch and rock beds, sod installation and removal, tree and bush trimming, seasonal color, and pressure washing. We show up on time, communicate clearly, price fairly, and leave every property looking sharp. Every project starts with a free estimate.
