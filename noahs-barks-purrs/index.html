About Us

Fort Walton Beach's Grooming & Boarding Home

Noah's Barks and Purrs is a locally owned grooming and boarding business on Racetrack Road in Fort Walton Beach. We handle both sides of pet care under one roof — full grooming for dogs and cats, plus overnight boarding when you travel. Our groomers, Stormy and Sunny, have built a following of regulars who come back month after month, including the big doodles and double-coated breeds other shops turn away. Clients tell us two things over and over: their pets come home smelling clean for weeks, and they get exactly the cut they asked for. We're proud members of the Fort Walton Beach Chamber of Commerce, and with 82 reviews at 4.5 stars, we've earned a lot of trust in this town.

Our Services

Grooming & Boarding Services

Full Dog Grooming

Bath, blow-dry, haircut, nails, and ears — the complete groom, finished the way you asked for it, not a one-size-fits-all trim.

Cat Grooming

Yes, we groom cats. Gentle brushing, de-matting, sanitary trims, and full grooms handled patiently by people who know feline body language.

Bath & Brush

A thorough wash, deep brush-out, and blow-dry that leaves your pet clean, fluffy, and smelling great for weeks — not days.

Breed Cuts & Hand Scissoring

Doodles, poodles, and double coats done right — breed-standard cuts and hand-scissored finishes, including the big high-maintenance coats.

Pet Boarding

Overnight boarding starting at $25 for pets up to 20 lbs and $30 up to 50 lbs, in a supervised space where your pet is looked after, not just housed.

Nail Trims & Quick Services

Nail trims, ear cleaning, teeth brushing, and de-shedding — walk-in-friendly extras between full grooming appointments.
