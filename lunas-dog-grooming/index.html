About Us

Wesley Chapel's Home-Based Dog Groomer

Luna's Dog Grooming is a professional, home-based grooming salon serving Wesley Chapel and Pasco County. Because we groom out of a calm home studio rather than a busy commercial shop, your dog gets one-on-one attention from start to finish — no stacked cages, no barking chaos, no rushing. That matters most for puppies, seniors, and anxious dogs who don't do well in a crowded salon. We handle full grooms, baths and brush-outs, breed-specific haircuts, de-shedding, nail trims, and ear cleaning. We're fully bilingual — se habla espanol. Above all, grooming here is done with love.

Full Grooming Service

The complete package — bath, blow-dry, haircut, nails, ears, and finishing touches, so your pup leaves clean, comfortable, and looking their best.

Bath & Brush Out

A gentle, thorough wash and full brush-out that removes dirt and loose coat and leaves your dog soft, fresh, and tangle-free.

Breed-Specific Haircuts

Scissor and clipper work tailored to your dog's breed and coat — or a custom cut styled exactly how you like it.

De-Shedding Treatment

Puppy's First Groom
